Comparison 2: PTCy vs ATG

Comparison 2 evaluates PTCy against ATG-based GVHD prophylaxis — a more deeply T-cell–depleting comparator. This comparison draws on paired studies after excluding PTCy+ATG combination, mixed, and unpaired arms.

Overall Survival

Show code 
tibble(
  M1 = post_c2_os$b_ptcy_binary,
  M2 = if ("b_ptcy_binary" %in% names(post_c2_m2_os)) post_c2_m2_os$b_ptcy_binary else post_c2_m2_os$b_eventsn_ptcy_binary
) |>
  pivot_longer(everything(), names_to = "Model", values_to = "log_or") |>
  ggplot(aes(x = log_or, fill = Model, colour = Model)) +

  scale_colour_manual(values = c("M1" = jama_navy, "M2" = jama_steel)) +
  labs(x = "log-OR (PTCy vs ATG)", y = "Posterior density",
       title = "Overall Survival — Comparison 2",
       subtitle = "k = 10, N = 12,851 · M1 OR 0.81 → M2 OR 0.82 (minimal attenuation)") +
  theme_jama()

M1: OR 0.81 (95% CrI 0.74–0.90), P(OR < 1) = 100%, τ = 0.51. Consistent OS advantage for PTCy over ATG, though attenuated from the pre-Block-9 estimate (0.69).

M2: OR 0.82 (95% CrI 0.71–0.94), minimal log-OR attenuation (~6%). Unlike the pre-Block-9 analysis (35% attenuation), steroid adjustment barely changes the estimate, suggesting that the OS benefit in C2 operates largely through non-GVHD pathways.

Non-Relapse Mortality

M1: k reduced to 3 after arm reclassification (from 5 pre-Block-9). The frequentist estimate is OR 0.63 (95% CI 0.30–1.34), I² = 0%. With only 3 studies, the CrI is too wide for reliable inference. M2 steroid-adjusted models were not fit due to insufficient k.

WarningC2 NRM Below Modelling Threshold

The pre-Block-9 C2 NRM (k = 5, OR 0.65 [0.43–0.97]) was nominally significant. Arm reclassification corrections reduced k to 3, and the estimate should now be considered hypothesis-generating only.

Acute GVHD Grade II–IV

M1: OR 0.58 (95% CrI 0.44–0.77), P(OR < 1) > 99%, τ = 0.55, k = 9. Concordant with C1 (OR 0.67), confirming aGVHD benefit is comparator-independent.

NoteCMV: From Direction Reversal to Depletion Gradient

In the pre-Block-9 analysis, a qualitative direction reversal was observed (C1 OR 1.41 vs C2 OR 0.77 [0.60–0.99]). With the expanded post-Block-9 corpus (C1 k = 22, C2 k = 13), C1 CMV remains harmful (OR 1.26 [1.07–1.47]) but C2 CMV has shifted to near-null (OR 0.92 [0.73–1.15]). The evidence now supports a depletion gradient (C1 harmful → C2 near-null → C2-sensitivity null) rather than a binary reversal. See Discussion for the full three-point gradient interpretation.

NoteIRM Across Comparisons

The C1/C2 IRM pattern mirrors the CMV depletion gradient: IRM is modestly elevated vs CNI+MTX (Bayesian OR 1.35) but reduced vs ATG (OR 0.90). This is consistent with ATG causing greater T-cell depletion–related mortality than PTCy. The C1 Bayesian–frequentist divergence warrants caution, but the overall picture suggests PTCy’s infection cost is offset by ATG’s even greater immune compromise.

```{r}
  annotate("text", x = -0.45, y = 0.05, label = "PTCy\nprotective",
           colour = jama_steel, size = 3, fontface = "italic") +

  scale_colour_manual(values = c("C1: vs CNI+MTX" = jama_navy, "C2: vs ATG" = jama_steel)) +

       title = "CMV Reactivation — Direction Reversal Across Comparisons",
       subtitle = "C1 OR 1.26 [1.07\u20131.47] vs C2 OR 0.92 [0.73\u20131.15]") +
  theme_jama()
```

::: {.callout-note}
## CMV: From Direction Reversal to Depletion Gradient
In the pre-Block-9 analysis, a qualitative direction reversal was observed (C1 OR 1.41 vs C2 OR 0.77 [0.60–0.99]). With the expanded post-Block-9 corpus (C1 k = 22, C2 k = 13), C1 CMV remains harmful (OR 1.26 [1.07–1.47]) but C2 CMV has shifted to near-null (OR 0.92 [0.73–1.15]). The evidence now supports a **depletion gradient** (C1 harmful → C2 near-null → C2-sensitivity null) rather than a binary reversal. See [Discussion](discussion.qmd) for the full three-point gradient interpretation.
:::

## IRM Across Comparisons
The C1/C2 IRM pattern mirrors the CMV depletion gradient: IRM is modestly elevated vs CNI+MTX (Bayesian OR 1.35) but *reduced* vs ATG (OR 0.90). The C1 Bayesian–frequentist divergence warrants caution, but the overall picture suggests PTCy's infection cost is offset by ATG's even greater immune compromise.
