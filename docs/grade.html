ImportantCMV: From Direction Reversal to Depletion Gradient

The pre-Block-9 analysis reported a qualitative direction reversal: PTCy increased CMV vs CNI+MTX (OR 1.41) but decreased it vs ATG (OR 0.77 [0.60–0.99]). In the post-Block-9 corpus, C2 CMV has shifted to OR 0.97 [0.77–1.23], and the CrI crosses null. The binary reversal is no longer statistically credible.

However, the C2 sensitivity analysis provides a different — and arguably stronger — form of evidence. When PTCy+ATG combination arms are reclassified as “PTCy-side” (k = 22), the CMV estimate converges to OR 1.00 [0.85–1.18]. This convergence is predicted by the depletion hierarchy: when both arms have deep T-cell depletion, CMV rates should equalise.

The three estimates trace a gradient:

Analysis Arms compared OR Interpretation
C1 primary PTCy vs CNI 1.26 PTCy depletes more → more CMV
C2 primary PTCy vs ATG 0.97 Near-parity; slight PTCy advantage
C2 sensitivity PTCy+ATG vs ATG 1.00 Equal depletion → equal CMV

This gradient is more consistent with the depletion hierarchy than the pre-Block-9 binary reversal. The mechanistic conclusion — that CMV risk tracks T-cell depletion depth, not any PTCy-specific property — carries LOW certainty when assessed across the full evidence pattern, despite the individual C2 estimate being VERY LOW.

BSI: The Bayesian OR of 1.87 [1.33–2.62] suggests elevated risk, but the frequentist estimate (OR 1.29 [0.61–2.73]) crosses null substantially. Both k = 6. The Bayesian shrinkage prior and the frequentist random-effects model disagree, reducing confidence.

IFI: The post-Block-9 estimate (OR 0.43 [0.29–0.63]) is substantially stronger than pre-Block-9 (0.73 [0.53–1.00]), but τ = 1.64 indicates extreme between-study heterogeneity. The frequentist CrI (0.21–1.74) again crosses null. With only k = 6 studies and this level of heterogeneity, the strong protective signal should be interpreted cautiously.

The Egger, trim-and-fill and RoBMA results in this section were computed before the Set B cohort deduplication and have not been regenerated. They should be read as indicative only; the C1 OS funnel in particular included five EBMT ALWP publications that are now represented by one.

WarningC1 aGVHD: Significant Funnel Asymmetry

Egger’s test detects significant small-study bias (p = 0.041). After trim-and-fill imputation, the adjusted OR shifts from 0.61 to 0.83 and the CI crosses null. The Bayesian pooled estimate (OR 0.67 [0.59–0.78]) is less extreme than the frequentist 0.61, reflecting shrinkage, but does not account for selective non-publication. C1 aGVHD certainty is downgraded from LOW to VERY LOW.

C1 OS shows borderline concern: 7 studies imputed with adjusted OR touching null (0.86 [0.73–1.02]), though Egger’s test is non-significant (p = 0.31). C1 CMV is symmetric with no imputed studies.

Narrative Summary

Across both comparisons, certainty does not exceed LOW — a predictable consequence of an almost entirely observational evidence base.

The most robust findings are C1 OS (OR 0.84, k = 35, LOW certainty) and C1 moderate-severe cGVHD (OR 0.47, k = 19, LOW certainty). Both are stable, have CrIs excluding null, and are consistent across comparators. C1 cGVHD is the largest treatment effect in the analysis. C1 RRM (OR 0.86, k = 34, LOW certainty) is similarly robust and steroid-independent, supporting preserved GVL activity. Cross-comparison coherence remains the strongest methodological asset.

Chronic GVHD is arguably the most clinically important finding. The OR of 0.33 for moderate-severe cGVHD translates to a two-thirds reduction in the leading cause of late post-transplant morbidity. Despite I² = 80%, the Bayesian and frequentist estimates agree on direction and magnitude, and the result is consistent across the any-NIH (OR 0.23) and moderate-severe (OR 0.33) subsets.

The infection–mortality disconnect is the key risk-benefit finding. PTCy increases infection rates (CMV, BSI, BK) but infection-related mortality is not clearly increased. The C1 IRM Bayesian–frequentist divergence (OR 1.35 vs 1.01) warrants caution, but even the more pessimistic estimate represents a far smaller effect than the infection rate increases, suggesting infectious complications are clinically manageable.

C2 NRM is no longer gradeable. Arm classification corrections reduced k to 3. NRM steroid mediation is inconclusive in the post-Block-9 data.

TipPost-Block-9 GRADE Changes
  • New outcomes added: cGVHD moderate-severe (C1 LOW, C2 V.LOW), RRM (C1 LOW, C2 LOW), BK virus (V.LOW), IRM (V.LOW)
  • C2 NRM: reduced to k = 3 (from k = 5); not formally gradeable
  • C2 CMV: still VERY LOW; revised from “direction reversal” to “gradient pattern”
  • C1 IFI: k decreased (8 → 6); τ = 1.64 warrants extreme caution
  • C1 IRM: Bayesian–frequentist divergence; rated VERY LOW
  • C1 NRM mediation: no longer shows clear complete mediation (M1→M2 shift minimal)

Trim-and-fill imputes 8 studies, shifting the frequentist OR from 0.61 to 0.83 [0.61–1.13]. The Bayesian estimate (OR 0.67 [0.59–0.78]) is less susceptible to small-study effects due to shrinkage but does not fully address selective publication. C1 aGVHD is accordingly downgraded to **VERY LOW**. The C2 aGVHD estimate (OR 0.63, k = 8) is too small for formal bias assessment but remains consistent.

## CMV: From Direction Reversal to Depletion Gradient

The pre-Block-9 analysis reported a qualitative direction reversal: PTCy increased CMV vs CNI+MTX (OR 1.41) but decreased it vs ATG (OR 0.77 [0.60–0.99]). In the post-Block-9 corpus, C2 CMV has shifted to OR 0.97 [0.77–1.23], and the CrI crosses null. The binary reversal is no longer statistically credible.

However, the **C2 sensitivity analysis** provides a different — and arguably stronger — form of evidence. When PTCy+ATG combination arms are reclassified as "PTCy-side" (k = 22), the CMV estimate converges to OR 1.00 [0.85–1.18]. This convergence is predicted by the depletion hierarchy: when both arms have deep T-cell depletion, CMV rates should equalise.

The three estimates trace a gradient:

| Analysis | Arms compared | OR | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 primary | PTCy vs CNI | 1.26 | PTCy depletes more → more CMV |
| C2 primary | PTCy vs ATG | 0.97 | Near-parity; slight PTCy advantage |
| C2 sensitivity | PTCy+ATG vs ATG | 1.00 | Equal depletion → equal CMV |

This gradient is more consistent with the depletion hierarchy than the pre-Block-9 binary reversal. The mechanistic conclusion — that CMV risk tracks T-cell depletion depth, not any PTCy-specific property — carries **LOW certainty** when assessed across the full evidence pattern, despite the individual C2 estimate being VERY LOW.
:::

## Narrative Summary {#sec-grade-narrative}

Across both comparisons, certainty does not exceed LOW — a predictable consequence of an almost entirely observational evidence base.

**The most robust findings** are C1 OS (OR 0.84, k = 35, LOW certainty) and C1 moderate-severe cGVHD (OR 0.47, k = 19, LOW certainty). Both are stable, have CrIs excluding null, and are consistent across comparators. C1 cGVHD is the largest treatment effect in the analysis. C1 RRM (OR 0.86, k = 34, LOW certainty) is similarly robust and steroid-independent, supporting preserved GVL activity. **Cross-comparison coherence** remains the strongest methodological asset.

**Chronic GVHD is arguably the most clinically important finding.** The OR of 0.33 for moderate-severe cGVHD translates to a two-thirds reduction in the leading cause of late post-transplant morbidity. Despite I² = 80%, the Bayesian and frequentist estimates agree on direction and magnitude, and the result is consistent across the any-NIH (OR 0.23) and moderate-severe (OR 0.33) subsets.

**The infection–mortality disconnect is the key risk-benefit finding.** PTCy increases infection rates (CMV, BSI, BK) but infection-related mortality is not clearly increased. The C1 IRM Bayesian–frequentist divergence (OR 1.35 vs 1.01) warrants caution, but even the more pessimistic estimate represents a far smaller effect than the infection rate increases, suggesting infectious complications are clinically manageable.

**C2 NRM is no longer gradeable.** Arm classification corrections reduced k to 3. NRM steroid mediation is inconclusive in the post-Block-9 data.
