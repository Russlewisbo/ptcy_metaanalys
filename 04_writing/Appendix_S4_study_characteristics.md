## Notes

1. **Patient imbalance (54,779 PTCy vs 122,979 comparator)** reflects large registry
   studies (EBMT, CIBMTR) where the comparator arm is typically much larger than the
   PTCy arm.

2. **Donor type asymmetry** is expected: PTCy is disproportionately used with
   haploidentical donors (47% of PTCy arms) while comparator arms are predominantly
   MUD or MSD (60% combined). This is a key source of confounding in the analysis
   and is addressed via meta-regression of haplo proportion as a moderator (see
   Sensitivity Analyses, Appendix S9).

3. **Steroid exposure differential** (PTCy 26.6% vs comparator 34.5%) reflects the
   lower GVHD burden in PTCy arms. This is the mediator variable in the M2 models.

4. **Conditioning balance:** PTCy arms show a slightly higher proportion of RIC (40%
   vs 34%) and correspondingly lower MAC (39% vs 48%) compared to comparator arms,
   reflecting the more frequent use of NMA/RIC conditioning in haploidentical settings.

5. **26 single-arm descriptive studies** (not shown above) are included in the
   systematic review but have no comparator arm and do not contribute to the
   meta-analysis.
